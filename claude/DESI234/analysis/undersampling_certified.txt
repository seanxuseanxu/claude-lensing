PSF CONVENTION: ss=2 -> ss=4 vs ss=2 -> ss=1
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rung conventions this run: {1: 'native', 2: 'subgrid', 4: 'subgrid'}
Per the module docstring (gigalens.jax.analysis.undersampling): supersample=1 convolves the NATIVE-resolution kernel on the detector grid ('native'); supersample>=2 convolves a subgrid_kernel at supersampled resolution before pooling ('subgrid'). ss=2 and ss=4 are BOTH 'subgrid' -- raising supersample 2 -> 4 changes only the quadrature (image is refined, integrated more finely) and does NOT change the PSF model. Going 2 -> 1 would cross the convention boundary and change the PSF model itself by convention_gap_max (5.022115295033183 sigma in the first, ss_ref=16 run) -- not reducible by supersampling, a different kind of change than refining the quadrature.
